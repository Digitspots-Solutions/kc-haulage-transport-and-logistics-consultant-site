About KC Haulage Transport and Logistics Consultant

Based in the heart of Lagos's industrial hub at Ajegunle, Apapa, KC Haulage has been a cornerstone of reliable transportation and logistics services for manufacturers and distributors across Nigeria. We understand the critical importance of timely delivery in the manufacturing sector.

Our strategic location near Apapa Port gives us unmatched access to import and export routes, while our experienced team and well-maintained fleet ensure your cargo reaches its destination safely and efficiently. From heavy machinery to sensitive materials, we handle every shipment with the professionalism and care it deserves.

We don't just move goods—we build lasting partnerships with businesses who need a logistics provider they can count on, day in and day out.

Comprehensive Logistics Solutions

Heavy Haulage Transportation

Specialized transportation of heavy machinery, construction equipment, and oversized cargo. Our fleet is equipped to handle loads of all sizes with proper securing and route planning.

Container Transportation

Efficient movement of 20ft and 40ft containers from Apapa and Tin Can ports to warehouses and manufacturing facilities across Lagos and nationwide.

Supply Chain Management

End-to-end logistics coordination for manufacturers, including pickup scheduling, route optimization, and delivery tracking to keep your operations running smoothly.

Warehouse Distribution

Reliable transportation between warehouses, distribution centers, and retail locations. We ensure your products reach their destination in perfect condition.

Fleet Leasing

Short-term and long-term vehicle leasing options for businesses with temporary or seasonal transportation needs. Well-maintained vehicles with experienced drivers.

Logistics Consultation

Expert advice on optimizing your transportation routes, reducing costs, and improving delivery efficiency. We help manufacturers streamline their supply chain operations.
